About the Gym
Our Story

We Didn't Start a Gym to Blend In.

Heavy Metal Cheer Gym started with a simple observation: the cheer world had a uniform. Not the one the athletes wore — the one the adults wore. Matching spirit wear, team colors, rhinestone fonts. Safe. Forgettable.

We grew up differently. Raised on Maiden and Metallica. More shows than recitals. We didn't stop loving that world when our kids found cheer — we just brought it with us.

HMCG is for every parent who felt out of place in the stands. Every athlete who wanted something that actually felt like them. Every family that shows up loud, proud, and wearing black.

Comfort Colors 1717 tees. Comfort Colors 1567 hoodies. Garment-dyed, soft, built to last. Not for everyone. Definitely for you.

01

The Athlete

All-Star competitors who are done looking like every other team in the field. HMCG gear is what you wear before and after the mat — identity, not spirit wear.

02

The Parent

The dad who's been to more Slayer shows than dance recitals. The mom who wants something she'd actually wear on a Friday night. Both found their section of the stands.

03

The Sound

Heavy metal never asked for permission. Neither did we. The music shaped the brand — the attitude, the volume, the refusal to apologize for taking up space.
